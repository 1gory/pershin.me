Lexbox

Learn English from the texts you already read. Save an unfamiliar word with its Russian translation, and Lexbox highlights it every time it shows up again, in any form, translation on hover.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store. Until then, install it from the ZIP on GitHub.

Words stick when you meet them again

Flashcard apps take a word out of its sentence and ask you to drill it somewhere else. Lexbox works the other way round: you save a word where you found it, and then you keep running into it, in the news, in documentation, in a forum thread, highlighted, with the translation one hover away.

Every one of those meetings is a small repetition you didn’t have to schedule.

What people use it for

Reading in English for work

Documentation, articles, pull requests, newsletters. Save the words that slow you down and stop looking them up twice.

Phrasal verbs and idioms

Save “put up with” once and Lexbox recognises “putting up with” and “put up with it” later. The tricky multi-word stuff is where it helps most.

Building an Anki deck

Export your words as CSV with the translation and the original sentence, and import them into Anki or a spreadsheet.

Studying in private

No account and no cloud. Your list of unknown words is nobody’s business but yours.

Features

Save in one click

Select a word or phrase and click the small + button, press Alt+S, or choose “Add to Lexbox” in the right-click menu.

Translation on your device

In Chrome 138 or later, Lexbox translates English into Russian with Chrome’s built-in translator. You can edit the result or type your own.

Context sentences

Lexbox keeps the sentence the word came from, together with the page title and a link back to the page.

Highlights in every form

Save “run” and Lexbox also marks “running” and “ran”. Hover any highlight to see the translation.

Your dictionary

Search, edit and delete words and reread every saved sentence. Back up to JSON, restore, or export CSV.

Completely private

No account, no analytics, no network requests. Your words stay in your browser on this device.

How it works

  1. Select a word you don’t knowClick the + next to the selection, press Alt+S, or use the right-click menu.
  2. Check the translation and saveIt fills in automatically in Chrome 138+. The first time, Chrome may ask to download its English–Russian language pack.
  3. Keep readingFrom now on the word is softly highlighted on every page where it appears. Hover it when you forget.

Questions

Which version of Chrome do I need?

Any recent one. Automatic translation needs Chrome 138 or later; in older versions you type the translation yourself and everything else works the same.

Which languages does it support?

English to Russian for now. The interface is in English and Russian.

Is anything sent over the internet?

No. The extension makes no network requests, and translation runs on your device. One honest caveat: highlighting uses the browser’s CSS Custom Highlight API, so a page’s own scripts can see which words on that page are highlighted. More in the privacy policy.

Can I move my dictionary to another computer?

Yes, with the JSON backup: export on one, import on the other. There is no automatic sync.

Where doesn’t it work?

Inside embedded frames, in PDFs, on browser pages and in the Chrome Web Store.
