Privacy Policy

My Little Plant · Last updated: May 28, 2026

Data collection

This extension does not collect any user data.

Information storage

  • Your plant's game state (chosen seed and pot, growth, water and health levels, and timestamps) is stored locally in your browser only, via chrome.storage
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No analytics, tracking, or monitoring is performed
  • The extension makes no network requests of any kind

Data usage

The stored game state is used solely to keep your plant growing between sessions and to advance the simulation while the popup is closed. This data never leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published at this URL and the "Last updated" date above will change.
