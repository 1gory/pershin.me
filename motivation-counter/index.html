Motivation Counter

Replace your new tab with a real-time counter. Track your age, count down to a date, or to the end of the year — a quiet reminder that time is passing.

⏱

Age counter

Watch your age tick up in real time, down to the millisecond. A quiet reminder that time is passing.

📅

Countdown modes

Count down to the end of the year or to any date you choose — a deadline, a birthday, an event.

💬

Daily quote

A new motivational quote every day from a curated collection of 1500+. Toggle it off if you prefer silence.

🔍

Optional search bar

Search the web straight from your new tab with your choice of engine — in the same tab or a new one. Off by default.

🎨

Make it yours

Six color palettes (three light, three dark) or follow the system theme, three fonts, and three counter sizes.

🔢

Tabs counter

See how many new tabs you have opened with the extension — a quiet tally that shows the time adding up.
