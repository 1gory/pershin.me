YouTube Milliseconds Timer

Millisecond-accurate timestamps, jump to any moment, an A→B interval timer, one-click copy, and a watch-time stats dashboard — all inside YouTube's native player.

⏱

Millisecond timestamps

Time displays in M:SS.mmm format directly in the player — no overlays, no clutter. Toggle on or off anytime.

📋

One-click copy

A copy button next to the timestamp grabs the exact moment instantly — great for notes, subtitles, or editing.

🎯

Jump to timestamp

Press G (or click the target button), type a time in SS.mmm / M:SS.mmm / H:MM:SS.mmm, and the video jumps there with millisecond precision.

↔️

Interval timer A→B

Mark two points with [ and ], see the exact delta down to the millisecond, and copy it with one click.

📊

Watch-time stats

Total time, today, daily average, a 7-day bar chart, and a monthly calendar heatmap — all stored locally on your device.

🎛️

Customize your toolbar

Show or hide each player-bar button — copy, milliseconds toggle, jump, interval A/B — so you keep only the controls you use.
